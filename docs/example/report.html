What the comparison measures

Each donor is one biological sampling unit. Retention is the fraction of that donor's supplied barcodes passing a fixed filter. Counts are original integer gene-expression UMI counts. “Early” and “Late” retain the study's author labels. Blood immune cells do not directly measure kidney tissue or future clinical outcomes.

Verification checks the original entries

Nextflow runs acquisition, validation, QC, export, and independent verification as separate donor tasks. The verifier recomputes metrics and accumulates original coordinate entries into every gene/donor/setting sum, then checks sparse AnnData counts, orientation, identity, and provenance.

Interpretation limits and next questions

  • Combined PBMC sums mix cell composition and expression.
  • Early/Late are author labels; no individual clinical values inferred.
  • Upstream barcode filtering is unknown; alignment and empty-droplet calling are not repeated.
  • Doublets and ambient RNA have not been removed.
  • Two fixed QC settings test sensitivity; neither is a universal quality standard.

Inspect flagged donors and threshold sensitivity before designing a downstream analysis. A future cell-type question requires separate annotation, doublet/ambient-RNA assessment, and a donor-aware statistical design. No differential-expression, biomarker, causal, or clinical prediction claim is made here.
